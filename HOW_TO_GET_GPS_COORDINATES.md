# 📍 How to Get GPS Coordinates for Your Store

## What are GPS Coordinates?

GPS coordinates are numbers that show the **exact location** of your store on Earth. They help customers find you easily on maps and enable accurate navigation.

### The Two Numbers:
- **Latitude**: How far north or south (Example: `13.4117`)
- **Longitude**: How far east or west (Example: `121.1803`)

---

## ✨ Method 1: Automatic (Easiest & Recommended)

### Steps:
1. **Click** the "Get My Location" button on the store creation form
2. **Allow** your browser to access your location when prompted
3. **Done!** Coordinates will be filled automatically ✓

### ⚠️ Important:
- Make sure you're **physically at your store location** when using this method
- Enable location services in your browser settings
- Works best on mobile devices or laptops with GPS

---

## 🗺️ Method 2: Using Google Maps (Manual)

### Step-by-Step Guide:

#### 1. Open Google Maps
Go to [https://www.google.com/maps](https://www.google.com/maps)

#### 2. Find Your Store Location
- Type your store address in the search box, OR
- Manually zoom and pan to your store location

#### 3. Get the Coordinates
- **Right-click** on the exact location of your store
- You'll see coordinates appear (like: `13.4117, 121.1803`)
- **Click** on the coordinates to copy them

#### 4. Paste into Form
- The first number is **Latitude**
- The second number is **Longitude**
- Paste each into the respective field

### 📸 Visual Example:

```
When you right-click on Google Maps, you'll see:

┌─────────────────────────┐
│  13.4117, 121.1803     │  ← Click here to copy
├─────────────────────────┤
│  Directions from here   │
│  Directions to here     │
│  Save                   │
└─────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 📱 Method 3: Using Your Phone

### On iPhone:
1. Open **Apple Maps** or **Google Maps**
2. Long-press on your store location
3. Tap on the **dropped pin**
4. Coordinates will be shown - tap to copy

### On Android:
1. Open **Google Maps**
2. Long-press on your store location
3. Coordinates appear at the top
4. Tap to copy them

## 💡 Tips for Accuracy

### Do's:
- ✓ Use the exact location of your store entrance
- ✓ Double-check coordinates on the map preview
- ✓ Update if you move to a new location
- ✓ Use "Get My Location" when physically at your store

### Don'ts:
- ✗ Don't use approximate locations
- ✗ Don't copy coordinates from a different address
- ✗ Don't use old/outdated coordinates

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### "Get My Location" button not working?
- Check if location services are enabled in your browser
- Make sure you clicked "Allow" when prompted
- Try refreshing the page and clicking again
- Use Method 2 (Google Maps) as an alternative

### Coordinates look wrong?
- Latitude should be between **12-14** for Oriental Mindoro
- Longitude should be between **120-122** for Oriental Mindoro
- If numbers are way off, try getting them again

### Can't find my store on Google Maps?
- Use nearby landmarks as reference
- Zoom in as close as possible
- Drop a pin at your exact location
- You can also use the street view feature
